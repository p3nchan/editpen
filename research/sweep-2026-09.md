# editpen pre-launch sweep, September 2026

Licence: CC BY 4.0 (see `LICENSE-RESEARCH`). Numbers only; no source text is redistributed. Ledger entry: S197.

## Question

How often does each editpen rule fire on text people wrote before ChatGPT, compared with text current AI models write from plain prompts? The answer set the v0.1 defaults (a rule is on by default only if it rarely fires on human text and fires clearly more often on AI drafts). It says nothing about who wrote a given text.

## Samples

**Human text**: 172 chunks, 256,756 Han characters, Traditional Chinese, dated January 2018 to 17 November 2022 (before ChatGPT's public release).

| Register | Chunks | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Casual | 71 | PTT boards (book, Tech_Job, movie, Japan_Travel, Boy-Girl, WomenTalk, marriage), 67 authors |
| News | 44 | Office of the President press releases |
| Encyclopedic | 57 | zh.wikipedia article revisions up to 2022-11-01, rendered as zh-TW (some source text may be mainland-written) |

Chunks are 500–3,000 Han characters, split at paragraph boundaries, at most two per document.

**AI drafts**: 192 drafts, half Claude (Opus 5.5 and Sonnet), half GPT-6.
- 128 topic-matched drafts (64 per family): one plain prompt per sampled human chunk, same register, e.g. 「請用繁體中文寫一篇約 800 字的網路論壇文章，主題：…」.
- 64 social-media drafts (32 per family): Threads posts, fan-page posts, blog posts, product copy, event announcements, reviews, LinkedIn-style posts and newsletter intros, e.g. 「請用繁體中文寫一則 Threads 貼文，分享…」.
- Prompts name only the topic and the genre; no style instructions.

## Results

Rates are per 1,000 Han characters unless stated.

| Measure | Human | Claude, topic-matched | GPT, topic-matched | Claude, social | GPT, social |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01a 「不是 X，而是 Y」 hits | 0.07 (15 of 172 chunks) | 0.19 | 0.63 | 0.79 | 1.28 |
| F26a paragraph lengths too even (share of texts flagged) | 16% | 42% | 67% | 22% | 22% |
| Dash marks (──, ——, or a single —) | 0.10 | 0.02 | 0 | 1.72 | 0 |
| M01b fires (≥3 marks and >2 per 1,000) | 1 of 172 | 0 | 0 | 5 of 32 | 0 |

Raw phrase counts (any position, not rule hits):

| Phrase | Human (256,756 chars) | Claude topic-matched (48,267) | GPT topic-matched (34,881) | Claude social (15,157) | GPT social (7,040) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 在當今 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 首先 | 18 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 其次 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 此外 | 40 | 13 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 總結來說 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

No AI draft contained a full 首先…其次…最後 chain. The experimental rules D02a, D04b, F02a, F05a, F07a, F08a, F09a and F11a produced no hits on any AI draft; F06a fired once (one Claude topic-matched draft) and M01a four times (one Claude social draft).

## Limits

- One prompt set and two model families; model versions change, so treat the numbers as a dated snapshot.
- Small samples, especially the social drafts (32 per family, about 470 Han characters each).
- The news register is a single institution; Wikipedia text is multi-author.
- Human chunks are longer than AI drafts; F26a and density rules are length-sensitive.
- These numbers describe how often patterns appear. They cannot be used to decide whether a particular text was written by a person or a model.
